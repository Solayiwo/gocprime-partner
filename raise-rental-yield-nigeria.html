How to Raise Your Rental Yield in Nigeria

If your rent has not changed in years while costs and market rates have, you may be earning less than your property is worth. Rental yield is simply the income your property produces compared with its value.

Claim My 5% Discount

Know what comparable properties earn

Checking what similar properties in your area rent for is the first step. GOC Prime says it tracks what comparable properties earn in your area.

Set and review rent fairly

Rent should match the real value of the property but stay realistic enough to keep good tenants. Reviewing it regularly avoids sudden large jumps.

Collect it on time and track it

Yield only counts if rent is actually collected. A dashboard that shows payments helps you spot gaps early.

Protect the asset

Prompt repairs keep the property attractive and protect its value, which supports both rent and resale.

Frequently asked questions

How often should I review my rent?

Many owners review at each renewal and compare it with similar properties nearby.

Will raising rent lose me tenants?

It can if the increase is too sudden or above market. Fair, well-communicated reviews reduce that risk.
