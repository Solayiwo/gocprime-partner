Tenant Not Paying Rent in Nigeria

Unpaid rent is one of the biggest worries for landlords. The longer arrears build, the harder they are to recover, so prevention and early action matter more than anything else.

Claim My 5% Discount

Prevent it before move-in

Careful tenant screening and a clear tenancy agreement set expectations from day one. GOC Prime lists both among its tenant management services.

Track rent from day one

Missed payments are easiest to deal with in the first week. Rent that is tracked and followed up consistently stops small delays turning into months of arrears.

Handle problems early

Talk to the tenant as soon as a payment is missed, record what was agreed, and keep every message. A property manager can do this for you so the relationship does not become personal.

Get proper legal advice for disputes

Rules on recovering rent and possession differ by state and situation, so speak to a qualified lawyer before taking action. GOC Prime says it provides legal and expert support when disputes arise.

Frequently asked questions

What should I do first if my tenant misses rent?

Contact them quickly, agree a written plan, and keep records. Seek legal advice before any formal step.

Does property management stop unpaid rent completely?

No service can promise that, but screening, clear agreements and consistent follow-up reduce the risk.
